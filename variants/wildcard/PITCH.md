# Wildcard: River + Sky

**What's different.** You land on **River** — one vertical timeline through your days. Overdue tasks float *upstream* in red, a pulsing **now** line sits between today's done and not-yet items, Today and Tomorrow are always full cards, and empty days collapse into a "5 open days" tick so a month fits on one screen. Tap any day to type straight into it. Inbox, Upcoming, Calendar, Sync and sounds are untouched.

**Why you'd want it.** It answers "what's next?" in one glance, no hunting across days.

**Experimental.** With no photo set, the background is an **ambient sky** — dawn, daylight, dusk, night, with a travelling sun/moon and stars — and panels become your 13% glass. A photo always wins. The first time it goes dark you get a one-tap "Turn off" (also in settings). A **mic** in quick-add speaks a task into the parser. Keys: `4`/`r` = River.
