# Sched · fun

**What's different.** Same app, same taps — completing a task is now a small event. The checkbox pops, a burst of confetti fires from it in the colors of whatever sound you picked (Destiny legendary = purple, exotic = gold, Fortnite elim = red, GW2 = green, "Off" = a quiet puff), and your phone buzzes if it can. The sidebar shows a daily ring (done ÷ due today), a streak flame, and a rank that walks Destiny rarities into CS ranks — Common → Rare → Legendary → Exotic → Silver → Gold Nova → Global Elite. Rank-ups get a CS-style badge slide. Clear everything due today and you get a "Day cleared" banner. After 6pm, Upcoming gets a one-line recap. Tap the ring for your stats.

**Why you'd want it.** Zero extra taps; XP and streaks are computed from your tasks, so iPhone and Mac agree without new sync. Undo still works.

**Experimental.** Rank names, the recap strip, and a couple of hidden title easter eggs ("barrel roll").
